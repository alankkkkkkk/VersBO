(2)~One posterior over reagent coordinates $z$, per-axis reliabilities $\lambda$ and GP hyperparameters $\theta$ explains both the experiments and the answers. Answers constrain only the named coordinates, so an untested ligand is placed along steric bulk before it is run, while its free coordinate stays uncertain (\S\ref{sec:posterior}). (3)~The next experiment maximizes qLogNEI averaged over coordinate samples. (4)~A free dimension that gains relevance is shown to the LLM to be named (\S\ref{sec:discovery}), and $\lambda_k$ falls towards zero on axes where the LLM is wrong; with every $\lambda_k=0$, \ours{} is LVGP. Each new round of comparisons is chosen by its mutual information with the next best experiment (\S\ref{sec:queries}).}
